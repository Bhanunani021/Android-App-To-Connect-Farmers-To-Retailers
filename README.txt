Project Title: Android Application to Connect Farmers and Retailers
Technology Used: Flutter (Dart)

1. Introduction

This document provides a comprehensive overview of the project titled “Android Application to Connect Farmers and Retailers.” The application has been developed using Flutter (Dart) as a cross-platform mobile solution aimed at addressing inefficiencies in the traditional agricultural supply chain. The system focuses on enabling direct communication and transactions between farmers and retailers, thereby reducing dependency on intermediaries.

2. Background and Motivation

The agricultural sector plays a vital role in economic development; however, the existing supply chain often involves multiple intermediaries, leading to reduced profit margins for farmers and increased costs for retailers. Farmers frequently face challenges such as lack of direct market access, price exploitation, and delayed payments. On the other hand, retailers encounter difficulties in sourcing fresh produce at reasonable prices.

This project is motivated by the need to create a transparent, efficient, and technology-driven platform that bridges this gap and promotes direct trade between stakeholders.

3. Problem Statement

The current agricultural marketing system is characterized by inefficiencies due to the involvement of middlemen, lack of transparency in pricing, and absence of direct communication channels between farmers and retailers. These issues result in economic losses, reduced productivity, and limited growth opportunities for farmers. Therefore, there is a need for a digital platform that simplifies the process of buying and selling agricultural products.

4. Proposed System

The proposed system is a mobile application developed using Flutter that facilitates direct interaction between farmers and retailers. The application allows farmers to register, upload product details such as crop type, quantity, and price, and manage their listings. Retailers can browse available products, compare options, and place orders directly through the application.

The system also incorporates features such as user authentication, real-time data updates, and secure data storage, ensuring reliability and ease of use.

5. Objectives

To develop a user-friendly mobile application for agricultural trade
To establish a direct communication channel between farmers and retailers
To eliminate intermediaries and improve profit margins for farmers
To provide transparency in pricing and product availability
To enhance efficiency in the agricultural supply chain through digitalization

6. Scope of the Project

The scope of this project is limited to the development of a mobile-based platform for facilitating direct trade between farmers and retailers. The system is designed to operate within a localized or regional environment and can be further extended to support large-scale deployment.

Future scalability includes integration with payment gateways, logistics systems, and advanced analytics for market prediction and decision-making.

7. System Architecture and Working

The application follows a client-server architecture where the Flutter-based mobile application acts as the client interface, and backend services (such as Firebase or REST APIs) handle data processing and storage.

Working Process:

Users register and log in as either farmers or retailers.
Farmers upload product details, which are stored in the database.
Retailers access the platform to browse and search for available products.
Retailers place orders based on their requirements.
The system updates both parties regarding order status and availability.

This architecture ensures scalability, real-time data synchronization, and efficient communication between users.
